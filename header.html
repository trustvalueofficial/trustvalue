Universal Header Navigation Architecture

This demonstration shows how your current TrustValue header can be expanded to let users navigate to all 18 sectors, individual brands, product categories, and research from any page on the website.

18 Sectors Hub Multi-Sector Brands Tab Products Catch-All Navigation Zero Breakage on Mobile

How This Architecture Solves Universal Sector Navigation

1. The "All Sectors" Hub Item

In the previous header, the navigation only showed Insurance brands. By adding an "All Sectors" high-priority button in the top navigation, users can open the 18-sector macro-cluster panel and immediately jump to Banking, Healthcare, Real Estate, Automobiles, or any other vertical with a single click.

Route: /[sector]
e.g. /insurance, /banking, /healthcare, /real-estate

2. Multi-Sector Brands Matrix

Instead of hardcoding only the 26 life insurers, the Brands mega menu now incorporates a lightweight sector selector rail on the left. Clicking any sector instantly populates the 4-column brand matrix without navigating away or reloading the page.

Route: /[sector]/providers/[provider]
e.g. /banking/providers/sbi, /automobiles/providers/tata-motors

3. Universal Live Search (Ctrl + K)

The top-bar search bar indexes brands, sector categories, and articles across all 18 sectors. Typing "HDFC" suggests HDFC Life and HDFC Bank; typing "Carpet" suggests MahaRERA Real Estate guides; typing "EV" suggests Automobile safety audits.

Dynamic Indexing: S = TV_SECTORS, B = TV_BRANDS
